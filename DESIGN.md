# Maz Works design

An independent studio run by one person: editorial, a bit playful, and plain about what it does. A cream page, big serif headlines, full-width colour bands that change the pace, real project images, one orange action. It should feel made by someone, not generated by a services firm. Evidence and plain words come before decoration, and originality never costs clarity, accessibility or reliability.

## Central idea

"I find recurring problems in businesses and build practical ways to make them easier." Every page should make that visible: a problem, the pattern behind it, the practical fix. Not an agency, not a corporate consultancy, not insurance.

## Tokens (`app/globals.css` :root)

Never add a new hex value in CSS. Use a token, or `color-mix()` of tokens. The palette is unchanged; what changed is how it is applied.

| Token | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `--cream` / `--surface` | Page and card background |
| `--peach`, `--sky-tint`, `--sun` | Full-width bands, note fills, zone tints |
| `--plum` | Dark band (text `--cream`), links, rules, icon strokes, selected states |
| `--ink`, `--muted`, `--line` | Text, quiet text, 1px dividers, 2px ink rules that open a list |
| `--signal` (= `--pop-orange`) | The one primary button, with `--ink` text, never white |
| `--pop-cobalt`, `--pop-yellow`, `--pop-pink`, `--pop-lilac`, `--pop-ink` | Pop moments only |
| `--ok` | Plants and success marks in drawings only |

Legacy names (`--sage`, `--sand`, `--wheat`, `--clay`, `--mist`, `--slate`, `--charcoal`) are remapped to the palette. Do not use them in new code.

## Pop rules

- Maximum 2 pop moments per page, chosen from: a cobalt hero band (text `#fff` or `--cream`, never ink), a yellow highlight on one key word, pop price bars (`/prices` only), one pink or lilac badge.
- Home uses exactly two: the hand-drawn orange swash under one word in the hero h1, and the orange primary button.
- Colour bands made of `--sky-tint`, `--peach`, `--sun` or `--plum` are structure, not pop. Use them to change pace between sections (see Layout). Never two bands of the same colour next to each other.

## Layout and typography

- Editorial, not a grid of identical cards. Prefer ruled lists (a 2px ink rule on top, 1px `--line` between rows), an asymmetric two-column split, one large image, and a single taped or tilted "note" per page. Avoid three equal cards in a row.
- Headings are Fraunces (self-hosted through `next/font`) at display sizes, `font-variation-settings: "SOFT" 100`, tight tracking. Body is Manrope. Small labels are mono, `.72rem` minimum, uppercase with letter spacing. No italics (not loaded). No external fonts.
- Section rhythm on the home page: cream hero, cream ruled list, sky band (work), plum band (tools), peach band (founder), cream steps, sky contact band. Each band is full width through `.rx-band`; content stays in the 1280px column.
- A hard offset shadow (a token mixed with transparency) is allowed on one featured artefact per band: the field note, the work screenshot, the plan. Not on ordinary cards.
- Motion is small and optional: the hero entrance, the swash drawing in, hover nudges on rows and arrows. Everything sits inside `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`, with a `reduce` rule that turns transitions off. Content is never hidden in the static HTML.

## Shared primitives (`app/reimagine.css`, `rx-` prefix)

`.rx-section`, `.rx-band` with `--sky`, `--peach`, `--sun`, `--plum`, `.rx-kicker` (mono label with a dash), `.rx-title` and `.rx-lede`, `.rx-split` (stacked, then two columns from 900px; set `--rx-a`, `--rx-b`), `.rx-tag`, `.rx-rule` (wave divider), `.rx-link` (plum link with an arrow). Pages opt in; nothing here restyles existing pages.

## Honest labels

Every demo, tool or example says what it is with an `.rx-tag`, and the label is the truth:

- `data-kind="client"`, REAL CLIENT WORK: built for a client and live for their customers. Solid block mark.
- `data-kind="internal"`, INTERNAL PROJECT: my own product or experiment. Diamond mark.
- `data-kind="concept"`, INTERACTIVE CONCEPT DEMO: a working example with made-up data, not a live service. Dashed border, round mark.

The text, mark shape and border style differ, so colour is never the only cue. Never present a prototype as client work. Do not describe a client build as "paid".

## Components

- Hero: serif line, one orange button, a secondary plum link, and a taped field note that shows one real problem and its fix (`.hm-note`).
- Four areas (`.mw-areas`): a ruled, numbered list, each row one link. Data in `app/page.tsx`.
- Work: a real screenshot from `/public`, labelled, with the limitation stated (text from `app/projects.ts`).
- Tools teaser and `/tools`: ruled index rows plus one featured demo. The table-picker plan art is drawn from its data (`MiniPlan`).
- Founder note: first person, true background only (finance complaints, Computer Science graduate, builds every job). No invented numbers or qualifications.
- Buttons: orange primary (`.button-signal`) for the main enquiry. A cream button on the plum band, plum links with an arrow elsewhere. Forms submit in plum.
- Buckets (`app/three-buckets.tsx`) and the short bio (`app/manazir-line.tsx`) are kept for `/what-we-do` and the trade pages. The home page no longer uses them.
- Footer (`app/site-chrome.tsx`): one row of links, the email and one legal line. The home page adds the pale-blue contact band above it.
- Interactive demos: real `<button>`s, `aria-pressed`, visible focus, status shown as text and a pattern as well as colour, works at 390px with the plan stacked above its panel, no network calls, no personal data.

## Readability

Body text 17px on desktop and 16px on mobile at least, prose max-width about 65ch, line-height 1.55 or more. No stylesheet text below `.72rem` (a test enforces it). Check at 390, 768 and 1440px.

## Voice and truth

"I" for Maz, "you" for the visitor. Plain British English, sentences of 25 words or fewer, no hype. Avoid: unlock, seamless, empower, revolutionise, cutting-edge, leverage. Never invent testimonials, logos or numbers. Prices come only from `app/offers.ts`. No AI in the offer. Never publish Maz's location or personal email.

## Audience

Owner-managers and operations or sales leads of small businesses and professional teams. They need to see the problem fixed, that it is one person, real delivered work, a starting price and one clear way to enquire. This is a client site, not a job-seeking portfolio.
