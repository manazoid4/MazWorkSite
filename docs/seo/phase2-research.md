# Phase 2 organic research (25 Oct 2026)

Method: 11 WebSearch queries, plus 4 page fetches (mapletree.studio x2, dotgo.uk, whito.co.uk). Limits (all **observed**): the search tool returns a result list, not a verified SERP position, and it is US-based, so **UK ranking order, rankings, volumes, People Also Ask and related searches are not observable**. Schema/JSON-LD on fetched pages is not observable (text only). Competitor prices and ratings are self-reported.

## Small-agency page structure (observed, text only)
- **Mapletree /web-design-nottingham**: H1 "Business and ecommerce web design in Nottingham"; sections for services, why-us, process, work, six-question FAQ ("What does a website cost?" answered with "quoted after a discovery call", no number). Single CTA "Start a project".
- **DotGO /location/derbyshire**: H1 "Website design in Derbyshire"; four priced tiers (£499-£899+vat, £34-£75/mo), reviews, instant quote; no FAQ.
- **Mapletree one-page blog**: H1 "Affordable One-Page Websites for Small Businesses"; £479 one-off stated; no FAQ.
- **Whito plumber guide**: H1 "How much does a plumber website cost in the UK in 2026?"; full price ladder (DIY £120-£250, freelancer £800-£3,000, agency £2,500-£10,000); no FAQ.

## Gaps competitors overlook
1. Fixed prices on location pages: only DotGO shows any; Mapletree hides its (observed).
2. Real FAQs: DotGO, the Mapletree blog and Whito have none; Mapletree has six but gives no figures (observed).
3. Missed-call/enquiry automation for UK trades: only one UK result (observed); no UK freelancer site seen (inference, limited sample).
4. "Pay only when it works" preview-first offer: no competitor observed offering it (inference).
5. Results are dominated by SaaS or vendor blogs on automation and booking queries, so a freelancer cannot win them (inference).

## Recommended new page targets (ranked, all inference from the observations above)
1. **Cheap one-page website for small business UK** (`/one-page-website`): Launch Page £295, price table, FAQ. Beats Mapletree's £479 on price.
2. **Affordable website design Derbyshire/Midlands service-area page**: price shown, FAQ. Must follow privacy rules (county only as a service area, no address or town).
3. **Missed call text back for UK small business / trades**: Starter Automation £99, with a UK PECR/opt-out note. Low observed competition.
4. **Website for plumbers/electricians UK cost**: a trade cost guide with Maz Works prices beside the market ranges. Whito's ladder shows the format works.
5. **Answering service for small business UK** (`/after-hours-receptionist`): angle "answers, books and texts back"; avoid price comparison with £14.99 plans.
6. **How much does a website cost for a small business UK**: honest guide; the topic appears in MR Marketing and Mapletree posts.
Skip: generic automation, booking-system and enquiry-management head terms (SaaS/enterprise-dominated).

Caveat: validate with Search Console and a real UK SERP check before building.
