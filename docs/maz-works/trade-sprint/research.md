# UK specialist niches: source-backed opportunity research

**Research date:** 11 October 2026  
**Scope:** ten niches defined in `app/for/niches.ts`, plus the four broad customer types in `app/customer-types.ts`. Repo rules and offer facts checked against `GOALS.md`, `app/offers.ts`, and `app/for/niches.ts`. No files or customer-facing pages other than this research note are in scope.

## How to read this

- **Source fact** means a linked regulator, trade body, or vendor documents the cited rule or capability. Vendor capability and performance statements are vendor claims, not independent validation.
- **Workflow inference** means a plausible sales or operating pattern suggested by sources and the repo. It is a hypothesis to confirm in discovery, not a claim about every UK firm or measured customer outcome.
- Software is a **keep/check-first shortlist**. Existing tooling may already do the job. **No connector, API, account access, or integration with a prospect’s stack has been verified.**
- Seasonal notes are hypotheses unless a source explicitly supports them. Ask about the business’s actual busy periods.
- Research does not establish market size, willingness to pay, conversion lift, or product fit. No outcomes are promised.

## Canonical offer and cost guardrails

These values are read from `app/offers.ts`; this note does not set or change prices.

| Custom Software | From £2,450 | For a defined job normal apps cannot handle; written spec and staged sign-off. Scope sets dates. |

| Add-on reference | Extra automation £99; extra website page £295; one-day set-up £49 each beyond package allowance | Exact scope and applicable inclusions must be checked. |

For one-off offers under £1,000, canonical payment is one invoice after the buyer has seen the work working and before go-live, due within 7 days. At £1,000+, payment is in signed-off stages. New paid apps, hosting, domain, SMS or other provider charges are paid directly by the customer; their amounts depend on provider and usage and must be checked and disclosed before commitment. No recurring fee is implied by a one-off offer. These are repo-stated offer terms, not independently audited commercial terms.

## Source quality, design references, and limits

### Design references requested

These are first-party references for product presentation and operating model; they do not prove the offer’s effectiveness or fit.

- [Plausible: privacy-focused analytics](https://plausible.io/privacy-focused-web-analytics) — its own description emphasizes a concise dashboard, limited data collection, ownership and open source. Treat privacy/compliance statements as Plausible’s claims, not legal advice.
- [Designjoy: design subscription](https://www.designjoy.co/) — its own model describes queued requests delivered one by one, direct founder delivery and flexible brief intake. It is a design-service model, not evidence that unlimited software work or its turnaround fits this business.
- [Linear: product features](https://linear.app/features) — its own product page groups work around planning, issue tracking, customer requests and insights. Useful as a reference for clear workflow hierarchy; no claim that Maz Works or prospects use Linear.

### Limitations and discovery checks

1. Evidence is mostly regulatory guidance and vendors’ own product descriptions. It supports rules/capabilities, not representative demand or measured ROI.
2. No customer interviews, market sizing, pricing study, competitor price comparison, app/API tests or data-flow audits were performed. Integrations remain unverified.
3. “Common pain” is inferred from the niche page copy and workflow. Validate with actual owners: current steps, volume, failure points, existing tools, who owns the data, and what a successful handoff means.
4. Seasonal factors are expressly tentative except Checkatrade’s roofing statement about peaks/troughs and planning around weather. Ask each business for its own monthly pattern.
5. Do not market automation as replacing professional judgement, required records, certification, allergen controls, animal welfare decisions or clinical care.
