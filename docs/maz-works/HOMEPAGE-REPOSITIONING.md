# Maz Works homepage repositioning and build brief

## Scope and success

Redesign the homepage messaging and journey only. Keep working routes, real enquiry delivery, trade guides, the B2B path, prices, examples, shared site identity and accessibility. The first viewport must answer what Maz Works improves, why Manazir is credible, and what a visitor can do next. Give owner-managed/small businesses and B2B/professional teams visible routes. Do not fabricate customers, outcomes, quotes, numbers or capabilities.

## Positioning and headline directions evaluated

Scores are 1–5; criteria: clarity, credibility, originality, conversion potential.

| Direction | Headline | Positioning | Scores C/C/O/Conv | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---:|
| A — practical problem solving | **Make the hard parts of business work better.** | Find where customer journeys or work processes break down, then make the practical improvement that fits. | 5 / 5 / 3 / 5 | 18/20 |
| B — customer journey | **Make it easier for customers to say yes.** | Improve the moments between discovery, enquiry, booking and service. | 4 / 4 / 3 / 4 | 15/20 |
| C — operational flow | **Less time chasing. More work moving.** | Reduce repeat admin and keep important work progressing. | 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 | 16/20 |

**Selected:** A. It most directly conveys the requested broad business outcome, supports both customer-experience and operational work, and leaves the solution open without sounding like a service catalogue. B and C remain strong supporting messages for specific sections and audiences, not homepage identity.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The homepage treats business friction as the starting point and practical improvements as the offer; it refuses the category-default agency service menu and a single-feature missed-call hero.

**OWN-WORLD:** Extend Maz Works’ existing restrained ink, paper, divider and signal-orange system. Use the distinctive working-note/field-report grammar: concise labels, numbered findings, editorial type scale and real inspectable artifacts; no new fonts, gradients, card wall, stock photography or decorative animation.

**STORY:** A business owner recognizes a customer or workflow problem, sees an appropriate outcome and honest evidence, understands Manazir’s relevant experience, then describes the problem through one low-pressure enquiry form. Small/local businesses and professional teams have clear routes within the same story.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Compact Maz Works navigation; oversized headline “Make the hard parts of business work better.”; one sentence naming customer friction and repeat work; one prominent “Tell me what’s not working” link to the sole homepage problem-first form; a concise Manazir credential line; beside it a three-step working note (find the snag → agree the right change → see the agreed example working). The missed-call walkthrough is lower as one example among several.

**FORM:** A working-note editorial composition, ranked first of the three homepage-opening structures; SEED REFERENCE `seed:b9eac5ff41f98dc1`. The user asked the agent to choose and implement the strongest direction. The alternatives were Two Clear Routes and Evidence First; neither replaces the selected shared problem-solving identity.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Build constraints and acceptance

- Homepage links both to `/for` (small/local businesses) and `/b2b` (professional teams).
- The missed-call example remains present and explicitly labelled as a demonstration, not a client result.
- Keep inspectable real work and the fictional complaints sample clearly distinguished; do not add testimonials or performance claims.
- Mention a £99 starting point only for an appropriate one-task build, with larger/specialist work explicitly priced separately; read prices from `app/offers.ts`.
- Use one clear homepage enquiry route and keep attribution, no-JavaScript fallback, keyboard access, reduced-motion behavior and phone layout working.
- Test first-viewport clarity, route links, form reachability, `npm run verify`, and rendered desktop/mobile widths.
