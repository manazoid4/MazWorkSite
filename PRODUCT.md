# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Business owners, operators and teams who want to improve how work gets done, customers are served, and enquiries turn into business. The homepage should give small/local businesses and B2B/professional teams clear routes without making Maz Works feel like several unrelated providers.

## Product Purpose

Maz Works identifies practical problems that cost businesses customers, time or confidence, then delivers an appropriate solution. Success means the buyer understands the problem Maz Works can help with, trusts the founder's relevant experience, and can take a low-pressure first step.

## Positioning

An independent, founder-led problem-solving business: understand where the work or customer experience breaks down, then build the practical improvement that fits. The solution may be a website, promotional content, automation, operational process/reporting, complaints consultancy, software or a tailored combination; these are means, not the identity.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Manazir Hussain is a Computer Science graduate with five years' experience resolving complex financial complaints and managing business accounts across the UK and Europe. He has handled difficult customer situations, identified recurring operational problems and contributed to improvements.
- Maz Works can deliver websites, automation, promotional content, business-process improvements, complaints consultancy, operational reporting and tailored solutions.
- Do not position Maz Works as a generic digital agency, an AI automation company, a web design service or a jack-of-all-trades. AI is not the offer.
- Prices are offer-specific. A relevant first task may start at £99; never imply every service or project costs £99. Canonical prices live in `app/offers.ts`.
- Preserve working enquiry, campaign attribution, navigation, accessibility, and real examples; do not fabricate results or testimonials.

## Brand Commitments

Maz Works is a distinctive, independent founder-led business. Customer-facing language should be natural, specific, confident and human; avoid jargon, exaggeration and corporate filler. Use existing truthful brand assets where useful. The site should feel deliberate and memorable, not like a template agency or generic AI design.

## Evidence on Hand

- Live product: JobFilter (`/work/jobfilter`), described on the live site as a product with paid plans; no customer results are published.
- Client build: Scrap Finance Partners (`/work/scrap-finance-partners`), with a live public site; no revenue or lead outcomes are claimed.
- Labelled practical examples include a missed-call follow-up walkthrough and fictional complaints deliverable samples. Keep examples clearly distinguished from client outcomes.
- No verified homepage testimonial or quantified customer result was identified in the current live page; do not invent either.

## Product Principles

- Start from the real problem and customer impact, not from a technology category.
- Recommend the smallest practical solution that fits; retain tools already working.
- Make the work inspectable and explain scope, limits and price honestly.
- Keep the founder directly accountable for delivery.
- Preserve flexibility to solve new business problems without a rebrand.
