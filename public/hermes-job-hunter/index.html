Hermes Job Hunter

A personal assistant for collecting job alerts, preparing applications and tracking their outcomes.

What it does

Hermes discovers vacancies, filters duplicates and prepares application answers from the operator’s profile and approved information. Every employer submission requires the operator’s explicit approval.

Why it connects to Gmail

Read-only Gmail access lets the operator import marked job-alert forwards and review proposed status updates from application emails. Hermes checks the connected mailbox against the configured dedicated address.

It cannot send email, edit messages or delete messages through this connection. Unmarked messages are ignored after their headers are checked.

Who can use it

This is a personal-use application for its operator. This page provides information about the app; it does not offer public registration or access to a mailbox.
